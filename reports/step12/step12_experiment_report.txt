13. EXPERIMENT FINDINGS
================================================================================
1. Effect of Entity-Aware Historical Features (Step 12A / 12B — E1 vs E0):
   - The three Store x Product lagged expanding features (Store_Product_Expanding_Mean_Lagged,
     Store_Product_Expanding_Std_Lagged, Store_Product_Lag1_vs_Expanding_Mean) were constructed across all
     100 Store x Product groups with zero leakage.
   - In TRAIN, their Pearson correlations with day-t Units Sold are near zero (+0.008154,
     +0.001878, +0.000622), because every Store x Product pair
     in this dataset has nearly the same long-run mean (~136.7) and standard deviation (~108.3).
   - Adding E1 to Ridge (E1-RIDGE vs E0-RIDGE) changes WAPE by +0.0119 pp
     and MAE by +0.0161. Adding E1 to HGB (E1-HGB-R0 vs E0-HGB-R0)
     slightly improves MAE by -0.0247 (from 89.1354 to 89.1107).

2. Effect of Controlled Tree Regularization (Step 12C — HGB-R0, HGB-R1, HGB-R2, RF-R1-REG):
   - Moderate/stronger tree regularization (HGB-R1 / HGB-R2) stabilizes HistGradientBoosting, improving
     Validation MAE from 89.1107 (E1-HGB-R0) to 89.0690 (HGB-R1)
     and RMSE from 108.7174 to 108.6745 (R² = +0.0001, outperforming Mean Baseline by -0.0208 MAE / -0.0154 pp WAPE).
   - Depth-constraining Random Forest (RF-R1-REG: max_depth=6, min_samples_leaf=50) eliminates the Step 10
     Random Forest over-dispersion error, reducing Validation MAE from 90.7605 (Step 10 unconstrained RF)
     down to 89.0703 (-1.6902 units improvement) and WAPE from 67.34% down to 66.09%.

3. Effect of Alternative Loss Objectives (Step 12D — L1 Absolute Error vs L2 Squared Error vs Poisson):
   - Optimizing L1 / Absolute Error (HGB-L1-E0-R0, HGB-L1-E1-R0, HGB-L1-E1-R1, HGB-L1-E1-R2) shifts model
     predictions from the conditional MEAN (~136.6) to the conditional MEDIAN (~107.9 - 108.1).
   - Because the target distribution is right-skewed (Validation Mean = 134.78 vs Median = 104.00), predicting near
     the median (~108.0) reduces Validation MAE from 89.0898 (Mean Baseline) to 86.0236 (HGB-L1-E1-R2,
     a reduction of -3.0663 units) and reduces Validation WAPE from 66.1016%
     down to 63.8265% (-2.2751 percentage points improvement)!
   - CRITICAL DIAGNOSTIC TRADE-OFF OF L1 OBJECTIVE:
     While L1 models achieve the lowest MAE (86.0236) and lowest WAPE (63.8265%),
     shifting predictions to the median (~108.0) introduces:
       (a) Systematic negative bias of -26.80 units per row (under-forecasting total volume by ~20%),
